About Us

You bring the ingredients. S.A.N.G.K.A.P. brings the idea.

Every home cook asks the same thing: what can I make with what I already have?
S.A.N.G.K.A.P. answers it by ranking recipes on how much of each one you can cook right now, so dinner starts from your kitchen, not from a shopping list.
