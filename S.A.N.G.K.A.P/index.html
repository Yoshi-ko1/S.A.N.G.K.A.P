    How S.A.N.G.K.A.P. works

    1. List the ingredients you already have at home. Nothing needs to be bought first.
    2. Every recipe is scored by how much of it you can make, weighted by how essential each missing ingredient is.
    3. Cook it, then rate it. Recipes the community rates highly rise into the Featured page.
